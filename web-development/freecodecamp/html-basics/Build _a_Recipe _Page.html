Creamy Chicken Pasta

This Creamy Chicken Pasta recipe is a go-to favourite that's perfect for busy weeknights when you want something comforting and delicious without the hassle. Ready in just about 20 minutes, It's easy to make, packed with flavour, and sure to become a family favourite everyone will enjoy!

Ingredients

  • Garlic
  • Cream
  • Parmesan cheese
  • Fresh herbs
  • Lemon Juice

Instructions

  1. Flatten the chicken breast down so it is all the same thickness. For best results, flatten it to about 1 - 1.5cm thick for best results.
  2. Season each side well with salt and black pepper. Dust with flour .
  3. Pan fry over a medium-high heat for 2 minutes each side until golden brown.
  4. Using the same skillet fry the garlic in butter over medium heat. Add the rosemary.
  5. Add the cream and bring to a very gentle simmer. Add the parmesan in between and Add ¼ cup of pasta water.
  6. Add the black pepper and lemon juice and mix in quickly. Let it simmer for a couple of minutes to thicken.
  7. Add your cooked pasta in and toss well. Let it gently bubble away in the sauce for 1 - 2 minutes.
