Welcome to Sri Lanka

Discover the "Teardrop of India," where ancient history meets tropical paradise. From the misty tea plantations of Nuwara Eliya to the golden shores of Mirissa, Sri Lanka offers a world of adventure in one compact island. Explore 2,500 years of culture, witness majestic elephants in the wild, and find your own piece of serenity in the heart of the Indian Ocean.
